Every trend has a home town, and half of them are lying about it. Pick one, watch it travel, then send it all the way home and see what each place has left to wear.

Real data, with gaps. 178 trends from the Day 9 fashion dataset. For 49 of them the birthplace comes from a sentence in the trend's own Wikipedia article, with the rival claims beside it, and 27 also say what the look was borrowed from. The rest are a rough guess: the first country (UK, US, France or Italy) our dataset records them in. Every card says which. A missing line means we found no source, not that the trend never got there.
